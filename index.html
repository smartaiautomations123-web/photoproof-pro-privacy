Privacy Policy for PhotoProof Pro

PhotoProof Pro ("we", "our", or "us") provides a mobile application designed for trade, cleaning, and maintenance professionals to capture on-site evidence and generate branded job completion reports.

1. Overview & Privacy Principles

We believe your business and field evidence belong to you. PhotoProof Pro operates on a local-first model. All job data, photos, voice notes, and customer signatures are stored directly on your physical device unless you explicitly choose to export or share them.

2. Information Collected and Permissions

The app requests the following device permissions solely to support core functionality initiated by you:

  • Camera: Used only when you choose to take before/after and issue evidence photos on-site.
  • Photo Library: Used only when you choose to select existing photos or upload your company logo for reports.
  • Microphone: Used only when you tap to record a voice note for job documentation. Background recording is disabled.
  • Local Storage: Used to save job records, generated PDFs, and offline databases (SQLite) inside app-private sandboxed device storage.

3. Location & Analytics

  • No GPS Tracking: PhotoProof Pro does not track, record, or store your GPS background location.
  • No Advertising or Tracking SDKs: We do not include third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or behavioural tracking tools.

4. How We Use and Share Your Information

Your evidence and reports are used solely to produce the documentation you request. When you export or share a PDF report, it is transmitted using your device's native operating system share sheet to the recipients you specify.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data resides on your device, you have complete control over retention:

  • You may delete individual jobs, photos, or voice notes at any time within the app.
  • You can purge all stored app data at any time from the Account screen by selecting Clear device data.
  • Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all local SQLite databases and cached evidence files managed by the app sandbox.

6. Security

Local files are stored in your device's secured application container. When PDF sharing or optional secure transmissions occur, industry-standard encrypted channels (TLS/HTTPS) are utilized.

7. Children's Privacy

PhotoProof Pro is a business tool intended for working professionals and is not directed to individuals under the age of 18.
